# PARITY — FAQ and how to use it

PARITY is a board. It is not a bank, not a wallet, and not a fund. It never holds your cedis, dollars, bills, or coins.

**If I put money on this, what happens — and is that number locked?**

## 2. The boards

**Currency triangle** — buy ask, sell bid, both loops, minus fees. Banners: TRIANGLE after costs / GROSS ONLY / NO TRIANGLE.

**Bonds / T-bills** — paper sits in your CSD account. Same bill two GFIM prices can lock after fees. Curve = not locked. Eurobond vs cedi = block. Bond future = off.

**Crypto** — BYO keys. We never hold coins. Ghana live send off. Two books = figure if both fill now, still not locked. Funding can flip. Exchange vs chain = block.

**Pipes we do not own** — mid vs bank vs payment-app print. We do not collect, convert, or pay out.

**Settlement** — interbank T+2, bureau cash today, CSD T+2, auction often T+1, PAPSS ~120s through your bank. Different clocks block Send.

## 3. Outcome before send

Read the banner. Read if both legs fill vs if the other side dies. Tick the box. Then arm. Most days: no trade.

## 4. Personal paper path

Connect → scroll four sentences → tick four boxes → Paper → scopes → Arm → both legs IBKR paper → tick outcome → Send. Kill drops our token. Money at IBKR stays at IBKR.

## 5. Desk path

Same boards. Human confirm. Live send off until counsel + licence map. Own books only.

## 6. What we do not do

Hold money. Run a pool. HFT burst. Treat bureau as interbank. Treat Eurobond as CSD. Honour a 60s payment-app rate as principal. Print MiFID authorised.

Ghana BoG + SEC are home law. MiFID is the grammar, not the licence.

## 7. If it looks profitable

Same instrument? Same date? After fees? Can you sell the rich leg? If the other quote dies, are you just long the thing?
