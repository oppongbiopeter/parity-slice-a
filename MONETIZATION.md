# PARITY — who pays, and the tutor

The board never holds money. We sell software and teaching, not a spread and not a pool.

## Tutor (paid in the live app)

Allowed: explain a banner; walk Connect / Settle / Ticket; list setups the boards already marked after fees; honour settings (FX / bond / crypto on or off).

Forbidden: send; invent a price; promise carry; call itself advice.

Preview can unlock the explainer locally. Live app: seat or subscription.

## Customers

1. Individuals with their own broker (GH / KE / ZA paper path).
2. Licensed desks — per seat, own books.
3. Training buyers — academies. No keys.

Not a customer: anyone depositing with us; EU retail in this slice; Nigeria IBKR live.

## Charge

Personal subscription (free Watch + paper; paid tutor). Desk seats. Institution licence. Training pack.

## Do not charge

Performance fee, payment for order flow, custody, live Ghana send as a paid toggle, a rate we honour as principal.
