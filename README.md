# PARITY

A phone-sized board that answers one question:

**If I put money on this trade, what happens — and is that number actually locked?**

It is software. It is not a bank, not a fund, and not a wallet. It never holds your cedis, dollars, bonds, or coins.

Open the clickable preview: [parity_app_preview.html](parity_app_preview.html)

## Who it is for

- Individual — PIN `123456`. Connect a broker you already own. Paper send only.
- Desk — bank `GCB` / staff `DEALER01` / PIN `123456`. Own books. Human RFQ.
- Watch only — calculator. Nothing sends.

## Boards

Currency triangle (bid/ask both loops). Rates/carry. Bonds on CSD/GFIM. Crypto BYO keys. Pipes comparison (mid vs bank vs payment app). Settlement clocks including PAPSS via *your* bank.

## Rule before send

Same instrument. Same clock. Outcome ticked. Paper armed. Ghana live keys and live crypto off until counsel. Most days: no trade.

## Pipes we do not own

Borrowed quote honesty from TradeRail, Divest Money Xchange, PAPSS-in-bank-apps, CrissCross: clock, all-in number, versus the bank.

Refused: wallets, collections in our name, guaranteed payouts, a USDC bridge we would hold, honouring a 60-second rate as principal.
