# PARITY — user guide

How to walk the preview and what each system is for.

## Open the app

1. Open `parity_app_preview.html` in a browser (keep `parity_app_1.js`, `parity_app_2.js`, `parity_app_3.js` in the same folder).
2. You land on **Who is this for?**
3. Pick Individual, Desk, or Watch.

### Individual
Country + PIN `123456`.

### Desk
Bank `GCB`, staff `DEALER01`, role, PIN `123456`.

### Watch
Continue without signing in. Calculator only.

## What to tap first

1. Triangle — change a quote or tap break one quote. Read the banner.
2. Bond — try each comparison. Watch LOCKED vs BLOCK.
3. Crypto — live send stays grey.
4. Pipes — type $2000. Compare mid / bank / payment app. We do not pay that cedi out.
5. Settle — paper + bureau cash should BLOCK. Both IBKR paper allows paper language. PAPSS is your bank's clock.
6. Ticket — Send stays dead until connect, arm, matching rails, and the outcome tick.
7. Account → Who regulates this — Ghana first.

## Connect paper (individuals)

Connect → scroll four sentences → tick four boxes → Paper → scopes → Arm → both legs IBKR paper → tick outcome → Send. Kill undoes the broker session only.

## Systems in one line

| System | Job |
|---|---|
| Triangle | Do three FX prices still agree after bid/ask and fees? |
| Bond | Same CSD line vs a curve vs a Eurobond vs a future that does not exist |
| Crypto | Same coin two books, or funding that can flip |
| Pipes | Honesty of someone else's quote — not their wallet |
| Settle | Will the two legs meet on one date? |
| Ticket | Say the leftover out loud before anyone sends |
| Connect / Kill | Borrow IBKR paper. Give it back in one tap |
| Law pack | Who actually regulates a Ghana user |

## Do not confuse us with

Chipper / CrissCross / TradeRail / Divest / PAPSS move money. IBKR executes. Your bank holds the T-bill. We sit in front and ask if it is locked.

## Stuck

Wrong PIN → `123456`. Send grey → settlement mismatch, not armed, or box empty. Live Ghana keys → counsel letter, not a setting.
